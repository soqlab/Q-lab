# Icons Required for PWA

Generate these two PNG files and place them in this directory:
- `icon-192.png` — 192×192px Q-Lab logo
- `icon-512.png` — 512×512px Q-Lab logo

Suggested design: Dark purple (#6366f1) background with white ⚛ atom symbol.

Tools: Figma, Canva, or run: `convert -size 192x192 xc:#6366f1 -fill white -font Arial -pointsize 80 -gravity Center -annotate 0 "⚛" icon-192.png`
